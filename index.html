Dispatch method and assumptions

Solar serves demand first, then grid imports up to 140 MW. Batteries discharge into the remaining gap. Each minute uses the power at the end of that minute for energy accounting. Usable MWh is deliverable AC energy. The model assumes no recharge, reserve requirement, or ramp delay during the hour. First shortfall times have one-minute resolution. The 455 MW municipal peak scales Tucson Electric Power’s 2025 retail peak to Belmont’s population. One 40 MW IT facility at an assumed hot-hour PUE of 1.34 adds 53.6 MW.
